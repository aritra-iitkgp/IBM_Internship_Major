IBM Telecom Churn Prediction

Machine Learning Based prediction of IBM Telecom customer churn

Introduction

Customer churn refers to the rate at which customers stop using a product or service. It is a critical business metric because high churn can significantly affect revenue, profitability, and brand reputation.

Dataset Description

In the telecommunications industry, understanding why customers leave is essential for designing effective retention strategies. This analysis explores the IBM Telco customer dataset to uncover key patterns and drivers of churn.

Are senior citizens more prone to churn?

Acquiring new customers is significantly more expensive than retaining existing ones. By identifying customers who are likely to churn, companies can take proactive measures such as personalized offers, improved service quality, or targeted support.

Through exploratory data analysis and machine learning models, we aim to answer important questions such as:

  • Which customer segments have the highest churn rate?
  • What services are most associated with customer retention?
  • How do contract type, tenure, and monthly charges influence churn?

Dataset Overview

The IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset contains information about customer demographics, account details, and the services they have subscribed to. The target variable indicates whether a customer has churned or not.

Key Insights

Initial analysis reveals that customers with month-to-month contracts, higher monthly charges, and shorter tenure are more likely to churn. Additionally, the absence of certain add-on services also appears to increase the probability of leaving.

In the following sections, we will explore these patterns in more detail using visualizations and predictive modeling techniques.

This study forms part of a larger machine learning project focused on building an end-to-end customer churn prediction system.
